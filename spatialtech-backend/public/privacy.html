Privacy Policy

How we protect your data and respect your privacy

Home / Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Spatial Heights Technologies. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal details (Name, Email Address, Phone Number)
  • Business information provided by clients
  • Messages or inquiries submitted through contact forms
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, device information)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide our services
  • Communicate with you regarding projects or requests
  • Improve our website and service delivery
  • Send important updates where necessary

4. Data Storage & Security

We take the protection of your data seriously. All information you provide is handled securely and only used for its intended purpose.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Your data may only be shared with:

  • Trusted service providers who assist in delivering our services
  • Authorities when required by law or legal processes

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Improve user experience
  • Understand website traffic and usage

You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party tools or integrations to enhance our services. These platforms operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them where necessary.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent at any time.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us. We are here to help.
