Who We Are

Spatial Heights Technologies

Spatial Heights Technologies is a geospatial solutions firm in Nigeria. We are dedicated to advancing the application of Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, and spatial data technologies across Africa and beyond.

Our work is driven by a simple conviction: that location matters. Understanding where things are, how they relate to one another, and how they change over time is fundamental to informing decisions across infrastructure, agriculture, energy, urban planning, environmental management, and many other fields.

At Spatial Heights Technologies, we translate that conviction into action. We deliver precision geospatial solutions that help organisations see their world more clearly, plan more effectively, and operate more efficiently.

Our Journey

How We Started

We recognised a critical gap in the African market: the need for an indigenous geospatial firm that combines technical excellence with a deep understanding of the African landscape.

What began as a vision to bridge that gap has grown into a dynamic team of passionate professionals, each committed to delivering geospatial intelligence that creates measurable impact.

We are young, but we are experienced.

We are a startup, but we operate to the highest professional standards.

Our Mission

To empower organisations across Africa with intelligent geospatial solutions that drive innovation, enhance decision-making, and unlock the transformative power of location-based insights.

Our Vision

To become Africa's foremost geospatial technology company recognised for technical excellence, local expertise, and an unwavering commitment to sustainable development.
