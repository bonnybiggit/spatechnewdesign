The Spatial Heights Technologies blog is where our team shares ideas, analysis, and expertise on the topics that matter most to the geospatial community. From practical GIS tutorials and remote sensing case studies to broader reflections on how spatial data is shaping Africa's infrastructure, agriculture, and environment this is our contribution to the conversation.

We write for GIS professionals, for decision-makers in government and the private sector, and for anyone curious about the power of location-based intelligence. We believe that knowledge shared is impact multiplied.
