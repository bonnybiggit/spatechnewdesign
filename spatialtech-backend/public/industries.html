Geospatial Solutions Across Sectors

Geospatial intelligence is not sector-specific,it is a foundational tool for any organisation that operates in, plans for, or manages the physical world. At Spatial Heights Technologies, we bring our expertise to bear across a wide range of industries, delivering solutions that are adapted to the unique spatial challenges of each sector.

Energy & Utilities

From oil and gas pipeline route planning to electric utility asset mapping and substation monitoring, the energy sector depends on accurate spatial data. We help energy companies and utilities understand what is on the ground, where their assets are located, and how to optimise their networks for efficiency and reliability.

Agriculture & Food Security

Nigeria's agricultural sector sits on a wealth of spatial data that remains largely untapped. We help agribusinesses, development organisations, and government agencies use satellite imagery, drone surveys, and precision mapping to monitor crop health, assess soil conditions, predict yields, and plan interventions.
